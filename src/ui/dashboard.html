    Counter position

    You can also drag the counter out of the way on the page. It stays there until you refresh the page, and double-clicking it puts it back.

    Block reels

    Turn Reel Meter from a counter into a blocker. A blocked reel page is replaced by a calm screen. Getting past it takes a wait and typing a sentence, and unblocking too often locks that app for hours.

    Not blocking right now

    Pick an app, a schedule or a focus timer below.

      How strict should it be?

      Pick how hard it is to get past a block. Unblocking too often locks that app for hours, so choose a level you can live with. A slip is fine: the lock only appears after repeated unblocking.

      Block by app

      A blocked app stays blocked all the time. Only its reel pages are blocked; the rest of the site works as usual.

      Limit and Shorts

      You have no daily limit yet. Set one and this switch takes effect.

      Focus timer

      Block reels for a set time on the apps you choose. Each press adds time.

      Schedules

      Block at the same hours every week. Overnight times such as 22:00 to 07:00 work, and belong to the day they start on.

      While a block is running, turning it off, shortening it or raising a hard limit needs the same wait-and-type challenge (Full restriction does not allow it at all, and nothing can be loosened during a lockout). Making a block stronger never needs it. Anyone can still turn the extension off in the browser, so this is for self-control, not parental control.

      Privacy and your data

      What Reel Meter does, what it cannot do, and how to check for yourself.

      What Reel Meter sees

      • Which site you are on and whether it is a short-video page
      • Whether a video is playing and on screen
      • Whether you touched the page recently, so it stops timing when you walk away

      What it never touches

      • Your screen, screenshots or camera
      • Video titles, channels, accounts, comments or messages
      • Your browsing history or any other site
      • The internet: it sends nothing anywhere

      Verify it yourself

        Pages of this extension are also blocked from making any network request by their security policy (connect-src 'none').
